TWO CHAMBERS. A SHARED FUTURE.

A voice on both sides.

The network evolves through on-chain proposals. Holders and miners must both approve — neither chamber decides alone.

01 / HOLDERS

Coins carry a vote.

Holders lock TCN until voting ends. Under mainnet defaults, voting lasts 80,640 blocks (about 14 days), participation must reach a 10% quorum, and yes votes must reach 66.67% of yes + no votes.

02 / MINERS

Blocks carry support.

Miners signal support in the blocks they produce. Under mainnet defaults, at least 60% of blocks in the voting period must support the proposal.

ON-CHAIN ACTIVITY

Proposals & decisions.

Loading proposals…

Some rules stay fixed.

On-chain votes cannot change the 100 million TCN cap, emission schedule, or proof-of-work algorithm. Approved changes follow an activation delay — 2,880 blocks (about 12 hours) on mainnet — so node operators have time to upgrade.

Take part from your wallet.

Review a proposal before voting. Voting locks the selected coin amount until the voting period ends. 

# List proposals and inspect one
thecoin-wallet gov list
thecoin-wallet gov show <proposal-id>

# Vote yes with a weight of 150 TCN
thecoin-wallet gov vote <proposal-id> yes 150
